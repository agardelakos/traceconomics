short: "People ranked by income and split into five groups of equal size: the poorest fifth, the second, the middle, the fourth and the richest fifth."

---
By default, people are ranked by their household's equivalised income, and each fifth holds the same number of people. Everyone in a household shares its income, so a household falls wholly into one fifth.

Two alternatives are available: households ranked by their total income, and people ranked by equivalised spending.
