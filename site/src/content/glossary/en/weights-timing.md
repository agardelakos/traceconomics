short: "Which year's survey a year's weights come from."

---
Rule N (the default) uses the survey of the year before, as the law asks weights to represent; until that survey's microdata are out, a first estimate uses the newest available, and the year is revised once. Rule F fixes the weights when the year begins and never revises them (survey of three years before). Rule U uses the same year's survey, as some studies made after the fact do.
