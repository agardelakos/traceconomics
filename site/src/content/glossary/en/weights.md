forms: ["weights"]
other: "Σταθμίσεις"
category: weights
short: "Each item's share of a group's spending: how much its price change counts in the group's inflation."
sources: [cpi-manual-2020, reg-2020-1148]
related: [plutocratic-weighting, price-updating, weights-timing]
---
If food is 27% of a group's spending, a 10% rise in food prices adds about 2.7 points to that group's inflation. Each group gets its own weights from the household budget survey, updated every year.
