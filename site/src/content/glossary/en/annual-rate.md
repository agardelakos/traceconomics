---
term: "Annual rate"
other: "Ετήσιος ρυθμός"
short: "The change in a price index compared with the same month a year earlier, in percent: the headline inflation figure."
sources: [hicp-manual-2024]
related: [annual-average, monthly-rate, percentage-point]
---
It compares like with like: September with September. That removes the seasonal swings of prices such as clothing in the sales.

As official statistics do, we compute it from the index rounded to two decimals and show it with one decimal.
