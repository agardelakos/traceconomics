---
term: "Standard error and error band"
other: "Τυπικό σφάλμα και ζώνη σφάλματος"
short: "How much a figure could differ with another sample of households; the band shows two standard errors either side."
sources: [eurostat-precision-2013, preston-2009]
related: [bootstrap, microdata]
---
Weights come from a sample survey, so a group's inflation would differ a little with another sample. A gap between two groups that stays outside the band is unlikely to be chance. The band covers the sampling of households only, not of prices, so it is a lower bound. The 2017–2019 surveys lack the information needed, so there is no band for the years that use them.
