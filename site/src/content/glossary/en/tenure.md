---
term: "Tenure"
forms: ["tenure"]
other: "Καθεστώς κατοικίας"
category: households
short: "Whether a household rents its home, owns it, or lives rent-free."
sources: [elstat-hbs-puf]
related: [actual-rent, imputed-rent]
---
We derive it from the survey's rent records: renters pay rent, owners record imputed rent for their own home, and rent-free households live in a home lent or subsidised. About one household in five rents.
