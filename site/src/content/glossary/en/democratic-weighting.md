---
term: "Democratic weighting"
forms: ["democratic weighting", "democratic weights", "democratic"]
other: "Δημοκρατική στάθμιση"
category: weights
short: "A group's weights as the average of each household's own shares, so every household counts equally."
sources: [cpi-manual-2020, ons-hci-method-2017]
related: [plutocratic-weighting]
---

The UK's Household Costs Indices use it. Our groups use plutocratic weighting, the standard for official price indices; on average the two differ little. The personal calculator works with one household's own shares, so for that household the question doesn't arise.
