short: "A number that follows the price of a basket over time, set to 100 in a chosen base period."

related: [annual-rate, chain-linking]
---
An index of 105 means the basket costs 5% more than in the base period. On this site the base is the average of 2025, so the 2025 average is 100.

The level of an index on its own says little; what matters is how it changes, for example over twelve months (the annual rate).
