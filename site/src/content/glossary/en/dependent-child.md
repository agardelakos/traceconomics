This is Eurostat's rule for the household budget survey, and we apply it to each person in the survey ourselves. A 20-year-old student living at home counts as a child here; a 20-year-old with a job counts as an adult. Households are then sorted into six types: one adult, two adults, three or more adults, each with or without dependent children. The age limit differs from the one in the equivalence scale (children under 14), which is about living costs, not about who depends on whom.
