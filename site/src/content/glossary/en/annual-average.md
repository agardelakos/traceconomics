---
term: "Annual average rate"
other: "Μέσος ετήσιος ρυθμός"
short: "The change in a year's average index compared with the previous year's average."
sources: [hicp-manual-2024]
related: [annual-rate]
---
This is the usual single number for a year's inflation. It is not the average of the twelve monthly annual rates, though the two are usually close.
