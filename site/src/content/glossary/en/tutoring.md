From 2026 ELSTAT's CPI gives tutoring no weight of its own, though it still collects its prices under secondary education. By default we keep tutoring in each group's basket and price it with the secondary-education index; the alternative leaves it out, as the 2026 CPI does. The two differ only from 2026.
