---
term: "Bootstrap"
forms: ["bootstrap"]
other: "Bootstrap"
category: surveys
short: "A way to measure a survey's uncertainty by drawing many new samples from the sample itself and recomputing everything."
sources: [rao-wu-1988, preston-2009, eurostat-precision-2013]
related: [standard-error]
---
We redraw the survey's sampling areas 1 000 times (the Rao–Wu method), rank the households into groups again each time, and rebuild each group's inflation. The spread of the results is the standard error.
