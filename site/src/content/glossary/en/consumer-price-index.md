---
term: "Consumer price index (CPI)"
other: "Δείκτης Τιμών Καταναλωτή (ΔΤΚ)"
short: "ELSTAT's monthly index of the prices Greek households pay, the national measure of inflation."
sources: [elstat-cpi-items, elstat-cpi-qr-2026, cpi-manual-2020]
related: [hicp, price-index, weights]
---
ELSTAT collects the prices of several hundred goods and services across Greece every month and combines them with weights from the household budget survey. The CPI covers the spending of households resident in Greece.

ELSTAT also publishes an index for each item (its table 07). Those item indices are the prices this site uses by default, combined with each group's own weights instead of the average household's.
