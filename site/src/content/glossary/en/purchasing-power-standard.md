Eurostat converts amounts into PPS with purchasing power parities: indicators of how much the same basket of goods and services costs in each country. Exchange rates can't do this job: they move with financial markets and expectations, not only with prices. Where prices are lower, a euro buys more than one PPS's worth, so the amount in PPS is higher than in euros.
