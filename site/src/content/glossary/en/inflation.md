Inflation is not the price of one thing going up: it is the average change across everything households buy, each item counted by how much of their budget it takes. That is why it is measured with a price index and a basket of weights.

This site measures inflation separately for groups of households, because they buy different baskets. When food and energy get dearer, a household that spends more of its budget on them faces higher inflation than the average.
