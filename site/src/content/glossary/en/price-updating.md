---
term: "Price-updating"
forms: ["price-updating", "price-updated", "price updating"]
other: "Αναπροσαρμογή στις τιμές"
category: weights
short: "Moving survey shares to the prices of December of the year before, since relative prices have changed since the survey."
sources: [hicp-manual-2024, elstat-cpi-qr-2026]
related: [weights, chain-linking]
---
Each share is multiplied by its item's price change since the survey year and the shares are rescaled to add up to one. ELSTAT does the same for the CPI. It keeps quantities fixed, so it slightly over-weights items that became dearer, as official indices do.
