---
term: "Imputed rent"
other: "Τεκμαρτό ενοίκιο"
short: "The rent owners would pay to rent the home they live in. No money changes hands, so price indices usually leave it out."
sources: [hicp-manual-2024, elstat-hbs-puf]
related: [actual-rent, weights, tenure]
---
The household budget survey records an imputed amount for every owner, so owners' and tenants' spending can be compared. It is about a fifth of all households' spending.

By default we leave it out and rescale the other shares, as the HICP does. Kept in, it is priced with actual rents, which can change inflation a lot: about +1.5 points for all households in 2025, when rents rose 8.4%, and about −1.8 points in 2022, when they rose only 3.7%.
