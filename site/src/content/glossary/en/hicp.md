For Greece, ELSTAT collects the prices and Eurostat publishes the index. The HICP covers all household spending on Greek territory, including tourists' spending, while the CPI covers resident households. Their weights differ. Their item price changes are almost the same from 2016, but differ more in 2011–2014.

On this site the HICP is an option for the prices. Before 2015 Eurostat's Greek HICP has item indices only for broader categories, so those years are marked "coarser detail".
