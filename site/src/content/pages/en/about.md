## What this is

An independent, non-commercial project that measures how inflation differs between groups of households, starting with Greece. Every number is computed from public data, and the method, the code and the source files are open, so anyone can check or rebuild them.

## Who it is for

Journalists, analysts, teachers and students, unions and consumer groups: anyone who needs to say, with numbers they can cite, how prices are moving for different households.

## What it isn't

Not an official statistic: ELSTAT and Eurostat publish the official figures, and bear no responsibility for these. Not a forecast. There are no ads, no cookies, no tracking and no accounts.
