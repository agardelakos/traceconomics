## What this is

An independent, non-commercial project that follows the economy with public data only, starting with how inflation differs between groups of households in Greece. More topics and countries will follow. Every number is computed from public data, and the method, the code and the source files are open, so anyone can check or rebuild them.

## Who it is for

Journalists, analysts, teachers and students, unions and consumer groups: anyone who needs to say, with numbers they can cite, what the official statistics show and what lies behind them.

## What it isn't

Not an official statistic: the statistics offices and Eurostat publish the official figures, and bear no responsibility for these. Not a forecast. There are no ads and no accounts. If you pick a light or dark theme, that one choice is kept in your browser so it holds from page to page; it is never sent anywhere.
