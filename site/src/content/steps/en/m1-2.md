---
title: "Why it isn't the same for everyone"
order: 2
---
The official figure uses one basket for the whole country. But every household's basket is different. A pensioner living alone, a family renting a flat and a well-off couple who travel a lot spend their money in very different ways.

So when some prices jump more than others, some households feel it more. If food and energy get much dearer, the households that spend a bigger part of their money on them are hit harder. Each country's overview shows how differently its poorest and richest households spend.
