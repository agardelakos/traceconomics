---
title: "What inflation is"
order: 1
---
Inflation means that prices in general are going up. Not one price: all the things a household buys, taken together.

To measure it, statisticians follow the prices of a **basket**: a long list of what households really buy, from bread and rent to bus tickets and haircuts. If the basket cost €100 a year ago and costs €105 now, prices rose by 5% in a year.
