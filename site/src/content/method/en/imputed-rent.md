## The default: left out

Owners don't pay rent, but the survey records what their home would rent for, so that owners' and renters' spending can be compared. No price index for it exists for Greece, and the HICP leaves it out ("currently not permitted", in Eurostat's manual). We leave it out too and rescale the other shares, as Bruegel's and the ECB's studies of inflation by income group do.

## The alternative: priced with actual rents

Imputed rent is kept and priced with ELSTAT's index of actual rents, the closest price measure available. This is our choice.

It changes inflation a lot, because imputed rent is about a fifth of the basket (more for pensioners and owners, little for renters). Rents rose 8.4% in 2025 but only 3.7% in 2022, so keeping imputed rent adds about 1.5 points to all households' inflation in 2025 and takes about 1.8 points off 2022.

## Not available

Valuing owners' housing by its user cost or by net purchases of homes would need a price index Greece doesn't have.
