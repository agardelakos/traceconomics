## The default: purchases

A price index measures the prices of what is bought, so ELSTAT's CPI weights are shares of households' money spending (in its words, "household final monetary consumption expenditure"). We do the same.

## The alternative: all spending

All spending adds what households produce themselves (vegetables, eggs, oil) and goods or services received in kind. Eurostat's household budget survey tables use this concept.

## How much it matters

Non-money spending is 5–10% of the poorest fifth's spending but under 2% of the richest fifth's. Counting it raises the poorest fifth's food share by up to 2.2 points. Imputed rent is a separate option and isn't affected by this one.
