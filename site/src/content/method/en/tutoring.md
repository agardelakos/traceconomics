## Why there is a choice

From 2026 ELSTAT's CPI gives "education not defined by level", mostly tutoring and language schools, no weight of its own, though it still collects those prices under secondary education. Until 2025 tutoring was weighted inside secondary education.

## The default: kept and priced with secondary education

Households still pay for tutoring, and how much differs between groups, so we keep it in each group's basket and price it with the secondary-education index, where its prices are collected. This is our choice.

## The alternative: left out

Tutoring gets no weight from 2026, as in ELSTAT's 2026 CPI. This is also our choice. For the first 2026 weights, which come from the 2023 survey in the older classification, tutoring can't be separated directly; it is removed using each group's own split in the 2024 survey.

## How much it matters

Before 2026 the two options are the same series. In September 2026 leaving tutoring out raises the groups' annual rates by 0.01–0.03 points.
